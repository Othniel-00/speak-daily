Speak Daily: your free daily English coach

Speak Daily helps you speak clearly and write correct English in about 20 minutes a day, with a morning and an evening practice session.

Learn grammar in 15 short units with quizzes, record one-minute talks and get AI feedback on clarity, pace and filler words, practise pronunciation with sound drills, build your vocabulary with spaced review, and fix your mistakes in a personal mistakes notebook. Chat with an AI coach by typing or talking.

Free, works offline, and installs on your phone: on iPhone open it in Safari and tap Share › Add to Home Screen; on Android open it in Chrome and tap Install app.
